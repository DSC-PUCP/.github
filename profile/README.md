# DSC PUCP 💻✨

**Developer Student Club – PUCP**  
A student organization at the Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP) that promotes software development through events, workshops, and collaborative technology projects.

---

## 🚀 Mission
To foster learning, innovation, and collaboration among engineering and technology students by creating projects that generate real impact within the university community and beyond.

---

## 👀 Vision
To become the leading student organization in technological support and development at PUCP, recognized for academic, organizational, and community impact.

---

## 📂 Active Projects
- **Valentine Web Gift** 💝: Interactive platform with Free, Basic, and Romantic plans, featuring personalized templates, quizzes, animations, and unique digital experiences.  
- **Landing Page DSC PUCP** 🌐: Official website created during an internal hackathon by the Production team.  

---

## 🛠️ Technical Organization
- **Repositories**:  
  - `valentine-web-gift` → Valentine Project  
  - `DSC-PUCP` → Organization Hub  
- **Branches**:  
  - `main` → Production  
  - `dev` → Development & testing  
  - Other branches → Templates or specific features  

---

## 📖 How to Join
1. Create your GitHub account.  
2. Request access to the **corresponding Team** within DSC PUCP.  
3. Accept the invitation sent to you.  
4. Review the contribution guidelines and start collaborating.
